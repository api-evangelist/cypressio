Your Accessibility and UI Coverage reports already tell you a lot. The harder part is getting that information in front of the right people at the right time. Starting today, Cypress Cloud webhooks can send two new events when a report finishes processing: * run.accessibility.completed for Cypress Accessibility * run.uiCoverage.completed for UI Coverage They join the existing run.completed event.
