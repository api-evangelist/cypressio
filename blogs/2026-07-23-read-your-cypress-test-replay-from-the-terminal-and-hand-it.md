These days, a lot of your day to day work runs through an AI coding agent. It reads your code, writes and runs your tests, and can gather the metadata around a failed CI run: which tests failed, the error, the stack trace. But the rich detail of what actually happened at the moment a test flaked or failed has stayed with you as a manual process.
